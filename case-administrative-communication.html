How it was designed Scope and approach

Scope: Training needs analysis, program proposal, session design, learning material, post-test, reaction form, observation form, and performance rubric.

  1. Diagnose the gap

    Identify administrative communication needs and translate them into practical training priorities.

  2. Design the program

    Convert the need into a competency-based proposal and 36-hour lesson plan with sessions, activities, OJT practice, and evidence requirements.

  3. Build learning support

    Prepare facilitator-ready material for confident, professional, and structured workplace communication.

  4. Measure quality

    Use post-test, reaction feedback, observation, and rubric tools to evaluate understanding, experience, and behavior transfer.
