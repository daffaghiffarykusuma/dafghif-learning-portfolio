Career Readiness Toolkit for Job Seekers

Practical tools to help early-career job seekers present their experience in a CV, LinkedIn profile, interview, and digital portfolio.

ATS CV Template for Job Seekers

A three-page ATS-friendly CV template with writing guidance, a fictional completed example, privacy reminders, and a final application check.

Job Seeker LinkedIn Checklist

A six-page checklist for role positioning, profile evidence, recruiter readability, networking habits, privacy, and profile review.

Job Seeker Interview Cheat Sheet

A six-page preparation and practice sheet covering introductions, STAR stories, common questions, interview logistics, grooming, follow-up, and recruitment safety.

Digital Marketing Portfolio Template

An 18-page portfolio template for presenting education, certifications, work experience, skills, and selected digital marketing work.

How it was designed Scope and approach

Scope: ATS-friendly CV structure, evidence-based LinkedIn profile development, interview preparation, and a digital marketing portfolio template.

  1. Clarify career direction

    Start with the target role, relevant job-post language, and truthful evidence from education, projects, internships, or other experience.

  2. Build application evidence

    Turn relevant work into concise CV bullets and a complete LinkedIn profile that recruiters can understand quickly.

  3. Prepare for selection

    Use structured interview practice, STAR examples, employer research, practical questions, and recruitment-safety checks.

  4. Package work visibly

    Organize education, experience, skills, certifications, and selected work into a reusable digital portfolio structure.
