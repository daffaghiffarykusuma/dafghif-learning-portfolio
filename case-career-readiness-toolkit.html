Case Study

Career Readiness Toolkit for Job Seekers

This case study groups four career-readiness artifacts that help early-career job seekers present truthful evidence across a CV, LinkedIn profile, interview, and digital portfolio.

Use case

  • Review a coordinated set of career learning materials for early-career job seekers preparing applications, profiles, interviews, and portfolios.

Scope

  • ATS-friendly CV structure, evidence-based LinkedIn profile development, interview preparation, and a digital marketing portfolio template.

Reviewer Context

What this case is meant to demonstrate, where the evidence is strong, and where claims stay bounded.

Use case

Use case: Review a coordinated set of career learning materials for early-career job seekers preparing applications, profiles, interviews, and portfolios.

Scope

Scope: ATS-friendly CV structure, evidence-based LinkedIn profile development, interview preparation, and a digital marketing portfolio template.

Evidence limit

Evidence limit: The artifacts demonstrate learning-material structure, practical guidance, safety prompts, and reusable templates. They do not prove hiring outcomes or guarantee ATS selection.

Case Flow

The work sequence behind the grouped artifacts.

  1. Clarify career direction

    Start with the target role, relevant job-post language, and truthful evidence from education, projects, internships, or other experience.

  2. Build application evidence

    Turn relevant work into concise CV bullets and a complete LinkedIn profile that recruiters can understand quickly.

  3. Prepare for selection

    Use structured interview practice, STAR examples, employer research, practical questions, and recruitment-safety checks.

  4. Package work visibly

    Organize education, experience, skills, certifications, and selected work into a reusable digital portfolio structure.
