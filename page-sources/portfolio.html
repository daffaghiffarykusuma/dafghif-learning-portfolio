DaGrow Mindset: Unlocking Your Talent Potential

By Daffa Ghiffary Kusuma
Publisher: Relation Ship Community (2024)

A practical playbook helping readers discover, develop, and maximize their unique talents through the Discover, Assess, Grow, Reflect framework. Combining real-life stories, science-backed insights, and hands-on activities, it offers actionable steps to break mental barriers, build skills, and create lasting impact. Written in a casual, conversational tone, it's ideal for students, professionals, or anyone seeking personal growth.
